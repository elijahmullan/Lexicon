How to play

Read each definition and enter the word you think matches it.

  • There are four puzzles every day, all set at baseline difficulty.
  • You have five guesses for each word. Spelling must be exact; capitalisation does not matter.
  • Each puzzle has one optional hint. Tap “Reveal hint” if you need a nudge.
  • Your progress is saved on this device. A fresh edition arrives at local midnight.
